This page explains the cookies the Fusion Football Club website uses and how you can control them.

## Cookies we always use

These are needed for the site to work, so they don't need your consent:

- **Manager portal login.** If you sign in as a team manager or club admin, cookies keep you signed in. Visitors who don't sign in don't get these.
- **Your cookie choice.** We remember whether you accepted or rejected analytics, so we don't ask on every page. This is stored in your browser (local storage), not sent to us.

## Analytics cookies (only if you accept)

If you click **Accept**, we use **Google Analytics** to see which pages are visited and how people find the site. This helps us improve it. Google Analytics sets cookies named `_ga` and `_ga_<id>`, which last up to 2 years. They don't tell us who you are.

If you click **Reject**, or don't choose, Google Analytics isn't loaded and these cookies aren't set.

## Changing your choice

Use **Cookie settings** at the bottom of any page to change your choice at any time. If you withdraw consent, we remove the analytics cookies from your browser.

## Questions

If you have any questions about how we use cookies or your information, please get in touch at [info@fusionfc.co.uk](mailto:info@fusionfc.co.uk).
